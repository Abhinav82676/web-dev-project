About me

Where my interest in code started, and what keeps me curious.

My interest in computers started with cricket. During a DRS review, I kept wondering about the control room behind the scenes: a whole room of screens, replay feeds and equipment working together so that one decision can be made in seconds. I wanted to know how all that technology fits together, and that question led me to programming.

I'm now studying Computer Science & AI at Scaler School of Technology in Bengaluru. I'm learning Java and data structures, and I build small projects so that I understand a concept before I move on to the next one.

I'm also curious about how large edtech companies run: how they build products, teach thousands of learners at once and manage as businesses. Outside code, I play cricket and enjoy thinking about how teams are managed. My goal is to go for higher studies in Computer Science.
